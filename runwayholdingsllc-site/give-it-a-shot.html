Give It A Shot.

A browser-based economics simulation for high school classrooms. Students step into the role of President, make real policy decisions, and watch the consequences unfold.

Join the teacher beta See the game
Purpose

Make economic trade-offs something students can feel.

Economics can feel abstract on a whiteboard. Give It A Shot turns the debate between free markets and planned economies into something students experience directly. Every decision moves the economy, and every result shows up in the numbers.

1

Lead the country

Students serve as President and each day face a desk of policy memos and executive actions to choose from.

2

See the consequences

Choices ripple through the economy, with a score and feedback tied to how the decisions actually play out.

3

Discuss and compare

Students defend their choices, compare outcomes with classmates, and connect them to what they are learning.

In the classroom

Built to fit into a class period.

Teachers can run Give It A Shot as a warm-up, a lab, or a unit capstone, and use the results as a starting point for discussion.

For teachers

  • Gives students a hands-on way to see how policy trade-offs shape an economy
  • Turns abstract concepts into concrete examples that anchor class discussion
  • Reveals how students reason, through the choices they make and defend
  • Supports both Core and AP / Advanced coursework

For students

  • Learn by doing: make the decisions instead of just reading about them
  • Experience that every policy helps some goals and costs others
  • See cause and effect play out, not just memorize definitions
  • Practice weighing evidence and defending a position
